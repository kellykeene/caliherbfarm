Fire Cider is a traditional folk remedy that packs a serious punch. We steep farm-grown horseradish, garlic, onion, ginger, and hot peppers in raw apple cider vinegar for a month, then strain and sweeten with raw honey.

## How to Use

Take 1-2 tablespoons daily as an immune tonic, or use as a salad dressing, marinade, or spicy addition to soups and stir-fries. Shake well before use.

## Ingredients

Raw apple cider vinegar, farm-grown horseradish root, garlic, onion, fresh ginger, hot peppers, raw honey, lemon, rosemary, turmeric.
