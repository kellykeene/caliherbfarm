Most echinacea on the market is root, which means the plant is killed to harvest it. This is the aerial harvest: leaf and flower cut from *Echinacea purpurea* in first bloom, which lets the same plants come back and keep producing year after year. We take the roots only when a bed is being turned over.

Cut in the morning after the dew lifts, dried on screens, and cut and sifted by hand. You will find purple petal throughout — that is what it should look like. Traditionally used for immune support at the first sign of a cold.

## How to Prepare

Steep 1-2 teaspoons per 8 oz of hot water, covered, for 10-15 minutes. Take frequently in small amounts at the onset of symptoms rather than in one large dose. A slight tingle on the tongue is characteristic of good echinacea.

## Ingredients

Farm-grown echinacea leaf and flower (*Echinacea purpurea*).
