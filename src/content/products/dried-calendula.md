We pick calendula (*Calendula officinalis*) every other day from June until frost — the more you pick, the more it blooms. These are whole flower heads, green base and all, because the resin concentrates right there where the petals meet the calyx. Petal-only calendula looks prettier and does considerably less.

If your fingers come away sticky from handling it, that is the resin, and that is what you are paying for. This is the herb we infuse into oil for our salve, and the one we recommend most often to people making their own.

## How to Prepare

**Infused oil:** Fill a jar loosely with flowers, cover completely with olive or jojoba oil, and let it sit 4-6 weeks out of direct sun. Strain well. Use as-is or thicken with beeswax for a salve.

**Tea or skin wash:** Steep 1 tablespoon per 8 oz of hot water, covered, for 15 minutes. Cool before applying externally.

## Ingredients

Farm-grown calendula flower heads (*Calendula officinalis*), whole.
