Our Chamomile Lavender Tea is a gentle and aromatic blend perfect for unwinding at the end of the day. Both chamomile and lavender are grown and dried on our farm for maximum freshness and potency.

## How to Prepare

Steep 1 tablespoon of tea in 8 oz of hot water (just below boiling) for 5-10 minutes. Cover while steeping to keep the aromatic volatile oils in your cup. Enjoy plain or with a touch of honey.

## Ingredients

Farm-grown chamomile flowers, farm-grown lavender buds.
