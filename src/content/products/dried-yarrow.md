Yarrow (*Achillea millefolium*) has been in the herbalist's kit for as long as there have been herbalists. We harvest the flowering tops with a portion of the feathery leaf at full bloom, when the aroma is sharpest, and dry them whole before cutting.

Traditionally used as a diaphoretic tea at the first chill of a cold, and externally as a wash or compress. Distinctly bitter and aromatic — this is not a beverage tea, and it is not meant to be.

## How to Prepare

**Tea:** Steep 1 teaspoon per 8 oz of hot water, covered, for 10 minutes. Best taken hot.

**External wash:** Steep 1 tablespoon per cup, cool completely, and apply with a clean cloth.

Avoid during pregnancy. Yarrow is in the aster family — skip it if you react to that family.

## Ingredients

Farm-grown yarrow flower and leaf (*Achillea millefolium*).
