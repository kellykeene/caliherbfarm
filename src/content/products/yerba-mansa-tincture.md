Yerba Mansa (Anemopsis californica) is a California native plant with a long history of use by Indigenous peoples for respiratory and immune support. We are proud to sustainably grow this special herb on our farm.

## How to Use

Take 1-2 dropperfuls 2-3 times daily. Particularly useful during sinus congestion or at the onset of respiratory discomfort.

## Ingredients

Farm-grown Yerba Mansa root (Anemopsis californica), organic cane alcohol, filtered water.
