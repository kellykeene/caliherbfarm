Nothing beats a cup of true farm-fresh peppermint tea. Our peppermint is harvested at peak menthol content and carefully dried to preserve its bright, cooling flavor and digestive benefits.

## How to Prepare

Steep 1 tablespoon in 8 oz of hot water for 5-7 minutes. Delicious hot or iced. Makes a wonderful after-meal digestive aid.

## Ingredients

Farm-grown peppermint leaf (Mentha x piperita).
