Bitter flavors are essential for healthy digestion, yet largely missing from the modern diet. Our Digestive Bitters formula combines traditional bitter herbs with carminative aromatics to stimulate digestive juices and soothe the gut.

## How to Use

Take 1-2 dropperfuls 15 minutes before meals. Place directly on the tongue to activate bitter taste receptors — the bitter taste is part of how it works!

## Ingredients

Farm-grown dandelion root, gentian root, orange peel, ginger root, fennel seed, organic cane alcohol, filtered water.
