Blue mallow (*Malva sylvestris*) has to be picked blossom by blossom, every morning through the bloom, and dried immediately or the petals brown. It is the most labor-intensive thing we dry, and worth every minute of it.

Poured over with hot water the flowers release a soft, slippery mucilage traditionally used to soothe dry throats and irritated tissue — and they throw an astonishing indigo blue that shifts to violet and pink when you add lemon.

Sold by the half ounce because these blossoms are almost weightless. A half ounce is a lot of flowers.

## How to Prepare

Steep 1 tablespoon of whole flowers per 8 oz of water, covered, for 10 minutes. For maximum mucilage, cold-infuse overnight in the refrigerator instead — heat is hard on demulcents.

## Ingredients

Farm-grown blue mallow blossoms (*Malva sylvestris*), whole and hand-picked.
