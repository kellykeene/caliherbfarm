Our Elderberry Elixir is a time-honored immune support formula made from elderberries grown right here on the farm. We combine fresh elderberries with raw local honey, cinnamon, ginger, and clove to create a delicious and effective daily tonic.

## How to Use

Take 1 teaspoon daily for immune maintenance, or up to 3 teaspoons daily at the first sign of illness. Can be taken straight, added to tea, or drizzled over yogurt.

## Ingredients

Farm-grown elderberries (Sambucus nigra), raw honey, organic cane alcohol, cinnamon bark, fresh ginger root, whole cloves, filtered water.
