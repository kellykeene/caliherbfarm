Lemon balm (*Melissa officinalis*) is famously disappointing when it is dried badly — it goes brown and tastes like hay. Ours is cut in the morning before the plants flower, when the leaves are highest in volatile oil, and dried at low temperature in the dark. It comes out green, and it still smells like lemon when you open the bag.

A gentle, everyday herb traditionally used to lift the mood, settle the stomach, and take the edge off a restless afternoon. Mild enough for daily drinking.

## How to Prepare

Steep 1-2 teaspoons per 8 oz of hot water, covered, for 10 minutes. Excellent iced with a little honey. Cover while steeping — the aromatics are the point.

## Ingredients

Farm-grown lemon balm leaf (*Melissa officinalis*), low-temperature dried.
