Our All-Purpose Healing Salve is a household staple. We slow-infuse farm-grown calendula and comfrey in organic olive oil for six weeks, then blend with beeswax and essential oils to create a rich, protective salve.

## How to Use

Apply a thin layer to dry, cracked, or irritated skin as needed. Great for gardener's hands, minor cuts and scrapes, bug bites, and chapped lips.

## Ingredients

Organic olive oil infused with farm-grown calendula flowers and comfrey leaf, local beeswax, lavender essential oil, tea tree essential oil.
