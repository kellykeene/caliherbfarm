Skullcap (*Scutellaria lateriflora*) is one of the herbs we are most protective of at harvest. We cut the aerial parts just as the small hooded flowers open along the stem, then shade-dry them slowly on screens so the color and aroma stay intact. Rushed or sun-dried skullcap loses most of what makes it worth growing.

Traditionally used as a nervine for a mind that will not stop circling — tense shoulders, racing thoughts at bedtime, the wired-and-tired feeling at the end of a long week.

## How to Prepare

Steep 1 teaspoon per 8 oz of just-boiled water, covered, for 10-15 minutes. Drink in the evening. Pairs beautifully with lemon balm.

## Ingredients

Farm-grown skullcap aerial parts (*Scutellaria lateriflora*), shade-dried. Nothing else.
